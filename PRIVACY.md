# Privacy Policy — Clear the Way

_Last updated: 2026-07-13_

**Clear the Way** is an offline puzzle game. We built it to collect as little
as possible — in fact, nothing at all:

- **No personal data is collected, transmitted, or sold.** The game has no
  accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics.
- **All progress is stored locally on your device** (browser `localStorage`):
  level progress, stars, coins, owned skins, and settings. Deleting the app
  or clearing site data removes it permanently. We cannot see it.
- **No network access is required.** After the first load the game runs fully
  offline.
- **The "ads" and "purchases" inside the game are demos.** They are clearly
  labelled, simulate the flow only, and never charge money, never load a
  real ad network, and never contact any server.
- **No third-party services** (SDKs, trackers, or cookies) are embedded.

If a future release integrates a real ad network or in-app purchases, this
policy will be updated first, and the store listing will disclose the
providers involved and the data they process.

Questions: open an issue on the repository.
